Our Story

Baked with heart,
designed with detail

Cake House Goregaon began with a simple idea — celebrations deserve cakes that taste as beautiful as they look. Every order is baked fresh in small batches and finished entirely by hand, from the first layer to the very last flower.

Whether it's an intimate birthday at home or a big celebration with family, we work closely with you to design a cake that fits the moment — in flavour, in colour, and in feel.

  • Freshly baked, never frozen
  • Fully customised designs & flavours
  • Easy ordering, right on WhatsApp
